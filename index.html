About Fact Finder

Your research, data and learning partner.

Fact Finder Research is a research and consultancy firm bringing together expertise in research, data science, statistics, software engineering, policy development, monitoring and evaluation, and professional capacity development.

We provide practical and customised solutions designed around the objectives, context, and information needs of our clients and partners.

Our expanded professional development offering supports early-career professionals working in clinical research and related disciplines through structured, credible, practical training.

We transform information into evidence, insight, capability, and action.

From research questions to actionable insight and professional capability.

Our multidisciplinary approach connects technical expertise with the practical realities faced by organisations, researchers, clinical research teams, and professionals.

Professional Training & Partnerships

Building the next generation of clinical research professionals.

Fact Finder Research partners with Contract Research Organisations (CROs), pharmaceutical companies, research organisations, academic institutions, and other industry stakeholders to provide credible, practical, and career-focused training.

Our training programmes are designed to support early-career professionals seeking to strengthen their understanding of clinical research processes, data standards, programming, documentation, and clinical trial data management.

  • Biostatistics for Clinical Research
  • Statistical Programming
  • Medical Writing
  • Clinical Data Management
  • Clinical Research Fundamentals
  • CDISC Standards and Implementation
  • Clinical Trial Database Management Systems
  • Data Standards, Quality and Documentation

Our Approach

From questions to evidence.

We structure our work around a simple principle: understand the problem, gather the evidence, find the meaning, and support action.

01 — UNDERSTAND

Start with the question.

We understand your objectives, context, challenges, and information needs.

02 — RESEARCH

Gather the evidence.

We design appropriate research approaches and collect relevant and reliable information.

03 — ANALYSE

Find the meaning.

We analyse and interpret information to identify patterns, findings, and insights.

04 — APPLY

Turn insight into action.

We translate evidence into practical recommendations, systems, training, and decisions.
